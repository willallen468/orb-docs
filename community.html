Orb Beta Community Guidelines

About Orb

Orb is operated by William Allen and is intended for people aged 13 and older. Provide accurate eligibility information and protect access to your account.

Share responsibly

You keep ownership of content you submit. Share only material you created or have permission or another lawful basis to share, including any necessary rights in images, recordings, music and other people's contributions. Do not impersonate others or expose someone's private information without an appropriate basis.

Check the audience before sharing. Public posts and profile information can be visible to others. The account privacy setting controls follow approval; it does not make public posts or profile information private. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. Do not use Orb as a secure vault for sensitive information.

Community rules

Do not share:

  • Child sexual exploitation material, sexual exploitation or non-consensual intimate imagery.
  • Threats, encouragement of violence, targeted harassment or hate.
  • Graphic abuse or pornography.
  • Scams, malicious software or content that infringes another person's rights.

Do not evade safety restrictions, misuse reports or interfere with other people's accounts.

Report a concern

Use Report and Block where available in the app. Blocking does not erase content already received. Leave a group if you no longer want to participate.

Send safety concerns, moderation appeals or rights requests to will@getonorb.com. Include the affected content or decision reference and explain the issue. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, authentication links or copies of illegal material. Avoid sending extra copies of private content.

A report receipt acknowledges submission, not a decision. William handles support and requests for review of moderation decisions. Decisions can be mistaken. Orb is not a continuously staffed emergency service, and no fixed response time is promised.

Orb may withhold or remove content or restrict an account to address violations, protect people or comply with law.

Privacy and beta information

The Orb Beta Privacy Notice explains data use, service providers, optional product analytics, separate TestFlight reporting, retention and privacy requests.

These guidelines describe expected conduct. They do not replace the applicable app licence or Apple's TestFlight terms: https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/testflight/
