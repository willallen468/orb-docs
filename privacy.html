Orb Beta Privacy Notice

About Orb

Orb is operated by William Allen. Contact will@getonorb.com about privacy or your information. Orb is intended for people aged 13 and older. Account and sharing features may be enabled in stages; availability is shown in the app.

Information used to provide Orb

Orb uses your email address, authentication information and account identifier to provide and protect account access. Eligibility information can include age declarations, an age band and how eligibility was recorded. Account services use this information to determine access and may also retain request metadata.

Depending on the features you use, Orb processes your profile, posts, selected media, messages, replies, saved items, follows and interactions. These support account features, sharing and discovery. Search text is sent to Orb's service to find results. Reports and support requests include the information you send and relevant account or content references. Content submitted for public review and safety reports can be examined with related account and submission information.

Public posts and profile information can be visible to other people. The account privacy setting controls follow approval; it does not make public posts or profile information private. Check the audience before sharing. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted.

Services used by Orb

Supabase provides authentication, database and media storage. Resend handles account and service email. Gmail handles support correspondence. PostHog handles optional product analytics as described below. Apple provides TestFlight distribution and beta reporting.

Connecting to these services exposes connection information, such as IP addresses, to them. Provider processing and retained technical records are separate from the information Orb deliberately includes in an analytics event.

Optional product analytics

Product analytics is off by default. If you enable it on a device and sign into a verified account, Orb sends PostHog account-linked usage events, session identifiers and limited app, operating-system and device-model information. Events cover actions such as opening content, saving, creating content and searching. Search events contain query length and result count, not the search words.

Orb's analytics event payloads exclude your email address, post or message text and precise location. Session replay and automatic screen or element recording are off. The analytics is account-linked, not anonymous.

PostHog is configured to discard client IP data from stored events, and its GeoIP enrichment is disabled. These settings do not mean PostHog never receives an IP address or that previously stored information has been removed.

Turning analytics off stops new capture and attempts to clear queued telemetry on that device. If clearing fails, the app reports the problem and offers a retry. Turning the switch off does not retract information already sent to PostHog.

TestFlight information

When you use Orb through TestFlight, Apple automatically collects crash logs and usage information and shares them with us. This can include app, device, session and crash information and your name and email address. Feedback you submit is also shared and may be associated with your name and email address.

We use this information to identify bugs, understand testers' feedback and improve Orb. Orb's optional analytics switch does not control TestFlight collection. Apple's notice explains its collection and retention: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/test-flight/

Device choices

The optional dynamic sky uses foreground location in memory on your device; its coordinates are not sent to Orb. You can change location permission in iOS Settings. System pickers provide access to photos or files you select. Submitting selected media sends that content to the service providing the feature. Orb does not request or upload your device address book. The accounts you choose to follow are stored separately.

Deletion and retention

Contact will@getonorb.com to ask about access, correction, deletion or other privacy concerns. Use account-deletion controls when available in the app, or contact us for help. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, authentication links or unrelated private conversations.

Orb's account-deletion policy provides a 15-day recovery period for recoverable requests. The recovery deadline is not a guarantee that every copy has been erased. Provider removal may continue afterward. Other people's content and records subject to an unresolved case or legal hold can remain.

The retention policy for minimal closed deletion receipts, closed enrollment records and closed moderation or support case records is 90 days after closure, subject to applicable closure conditions and documented holds. The policy calls for delivery contact details to be cleared after verified delivery, or 30 days after a recorded terminal delivery failure. An uncertain send is not treated as delivered or failed.

These periods do not describe every provider log, backup, email copy or beta diagnostic record. Support correspondence can remain in Gmail separately from closed case records. Apple's retention periods do not apply to any TestFlight feedback or reports Orb keeps outside Apple's tools. No fixed deletion deadline is promised here for those separate copies. Contact will@getonorb.com to ask about or request deletion of this information.

Orb's PostHog project uses a one-year event-retention window. Expired events are removed through PostHog's cleanup process rather than at an exact anniversary. Person profiles and properties are retained separately until deleted. Turning analytics off does not delete this previously collected information.

Supabase's published Free-plan schedule provides one day for API/database logs and one hour for Auth Audit Logs. Orb's Free plan does not include project backups. These facts do not establish that no provider-internal copies exist. Resend's published schedule for Free, Pro and Scale retains email and log data for 30 days, with backups persisting for seven days. Provider schedules are not guarantees of immediate erasure.

Documentation website

The documentation site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitor IP addresses for security. This website description applies to Orb's documentation site, not the separate getonorb.com homepage.

Privacy questions

Email will@getonorb.com with your request and the affected account or content reference, if relevant. William handles support and requests for review. Orb is not a continuously staffed emergency service.
