Orb Support

Need help with Orb? Contact William Allen at will@getonorb.com. Include your app version, what happened and what you expected. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, authentication links or unrelated private conversations.

For a safety concern, include the relevant content or decision reference when available. To question a moderation decision, explain the issue without sending extra copies of private content.

William handles support and requests for review of moderation decisions. This is not a continuously staffed emergency service. No fixed response time is promised.
